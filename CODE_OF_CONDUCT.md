# Code of conduct

Taskbar Companions follows the [Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).

In short: be kind and respectful, assume good intent, welcome newcomers, and keep criticism about the work, not the person. Harassment, discrimination, personal attacks, and sharing someone's private information without their permission aren't tolerated.

This applies in all project spaces, such as issues and pull requests, and when you represent the project elsewhere.

## Reporting

The maintainer, [@levi2111](https://github.com/levi2111), handles reports and will keep them as confidential as possible.

- To report a comment, issue or pull request, use GitHub's **Report content** option in its **…** menu. That reaches GitHub's staff as well.
- For anything else, open an issue that says you'd like to be contacted privately, without details, and the maintainer will reach out.

Reports are reviewed promptly. Depending on what happened, responses range from a private word to removing content or blocking someone from the project, as the Contributor Covenant's enforcement guidelines describe.
